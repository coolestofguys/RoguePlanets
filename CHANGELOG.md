# Changelog

## 1.0.1

- New: the in-game codex now has entries for the mod's market conditions and planet types. Each one stays locked until you find it: survey a world that has it, dock at a colony that has it, or visit a world of that type.
- Changed: a few of the stranger things that happen out there are now rarer, no longer grow more frequent over time, and stop once their story has run its course.
- Save-compatible with 1.0.0: just replace the mod folder.

## 1.0.0 (first public release)

- Six new planet types spawn across the frontier: Nocturnal, Halite, Chthonian, Sub-Brown Dwarf, Snowball and Sundered.
- A few one-of-a-kind worlds per sector.
- Over thirty new market conditions, many of them industry synergies.
- Something else, for those who explore far enough.
- Compatible with Starsector 0.98.5a. Requires a new game.
