# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (first public release)

- Six new planet types spawn across the frontier: Nocturnal, Halite, Chthonian, Sub-Brown Dwarf, Snowball and Sundered.
- A few one-of-a-kind worlds per sector.
- Over thirty new market conditions, many of them industry synergies.
- Something else, for those who explore far enough.
- Compatible with Starsector 0.98.5a. Requires a new game.
